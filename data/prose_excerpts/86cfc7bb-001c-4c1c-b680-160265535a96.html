Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from utility inspectors to first responders, soldiers in battlefield scenarios and beyond.

About the Role:

We're looking for a Senior Frontend Engineer to lead development of real-time, high-impact applications used by drone pilots, first responders, and mission commanders. You’ll be a key contributor in shaping the future of drones as critical infrastructure - building intuitive, resilient, and high-performance interfaces that deliver mission-critical video, telemetry, and control.

How You’ll Make an Impact:

  • Innovate on UI/UX in Skydio’s real time browser flight experience.

  • Develop mission and mapping workflows in 3D with geospatial data

  • Improve browser performance through process splitting and web workers

  • Help set engineering culture around best practices through CRs and tech talks

  • Enable pilots to execute complex multi-drone operations with ease.

  • Collaborate with autonomy and iterate on features with in field feedback.

What Makes You a Good Fit:

  • 5+ years of experience building and shipping customer-facing frontend applications.

  • Strong expertise in modern web development (React, TypeScript, GraphQL, WebGL/Three.js).

  • Proven ability to design systems that scale for performance, reliability, and usability.

  • Strong product intuition and empathy for end-users operating in high-stakes environments.

  • Demonstrated experience working across teams to deliver complex, integrated features from concept to production.

Bonus Points:

  • Experience working with geospatial data visualization (Mapbox, Cesium, or similar).

  • Familiarity with photogrammetry, scanning, mapping, or rendering pipelines.

  • Experience integrating frontend with cloud-based services (AWS, Temporal, Kubernetes).

  • Exposure to autonomy or robotics platforms.)

Work Location:

This role is a hybrid position, requiring you to work from our San Mateo, California office three days per week. While we are flexible regarding which days you come in, all employees are expected to be able to commute to the office three times a week or be open to relocating closer.
