## Skydio

The autonomy incumbent, now an industrial play. [Adam Bry](https://www.skydio.com/blog/skydio-series-f)'s pitch this month was that Skydio is profitable enough that it had to **turn investors away**: the [Series F closed at only $110M](https://dronedj.com/2026/04/30/skydio-drone-funding-skyforge-us/) "despite investor demand to put substantially more in," and the company is generating "hundreds of millions in annual revenue" with margins funding ongoing ops. That's the unlock for the parallel announcement: [**SkyForge**](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/skydio-commits-3-5-billion-to-expand-us-manufacturing-and-secure-american-drone-leadership-302752473.html), a 5-year, $3.5B U.S. manufacturing push (5x-larger facility, 2,000 new Skydio jobs, 3,000 supplier jobs, $1B to domestic suppliers). Skydio is treating the DJI ban as a multi-year industrial opportunity, not a 12-month sales spike.

The product story is **X10 + Dock + DFR Command**, glued to [Axon](https://www.axon.com/dfr). The April 2026 month surfaced four municipal pilots/contracts running this stack: [Bloomington MN's two-week X10 + Dock pilot](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/19/bloomington-skydio-x10-dfr-drones-response/), [Leander TX live-911 trial](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/06/leander-skydio-drones-911/), [Fresno PD catching Dick's Sporting Goods thieves from a rooftop dock](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/14/fresno-skydio-x10-drones-thieves/), and a missing-Alzheimer's-patient save reported by [ADrones](https://a-drones.com/news/skydio-x10-drone-finds-missing-alzheimer-s-patient-in-24-minutes/). The reference stat is Miami Beach PD: **41% of calls cleared without an officer**, drone on scene first in **80% of cases**. DFR Command has now processed **10M+ calls for service** across all agencies running it. Axon's [SkySwap program](https://www.axon.com/dfr) is explicitly pitched as the path to swap out DJI for NDAA-compliant Skydio under the "Restoring American Airspace Sovereignty" executive order.

Defense is the other Skydio story: a multi-million-dollar [USAF X10D EOD follow-on contract expansion](https://www.skydio.com/blog/us-air-force-x10d-eod-follow-on-contract), the [$52M+ U.S. Army X10D order](https://www.skydio.com/blog/USAF-Awards-Skydio-Initial-Contracts-to-Bring-Advanced-Autonomy-to-Mission-Critical-specialties) (largest single-vendor tactical sUAS order in Army history), and a U.S. Air Forces Central selection for Dock deployments at Middle East airbases. On the engineering side, the [TipRanks weekly recap](https://www.tipranks.com/news/private-companies/skydio-weekly-recap) flagged a new cloud-based multi-dock airspace deconfliction system - "10,000+ airspace conflicts resolved, zero collisions, median delay under 5 seconds" - that's the fleet-management story that has to work before any city can run 20+ docks. **Key counter-signal:** [DroneXL's Fulshear PD writeup](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/20/fulshear-pd-dji-matrice-350-rtk-x10/) on why Fulshear chose the DJI Matrice 350 RTK over the X10 - X10 + Dock is "north of $50K" once integrated, X10 alone is $20-25K, modular payloads on the M350 RTK win the price-per-capability fight when a department isn't NDAA-constrained. Skydio's moat is the autonomy + Axon integration, not the spec sheet.

## Brinc

The Guardian launch is the single biggest product event of the window. On [March 24, 2026](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/brinc-unveils-guardian-a-starlink-connected-drone-that-could-replace-the-police-helicopter/), Brinc unveiled Guardian at its new Queen Anne (Seattle) factory: world's first Starlink-connected DFR drone, **62-min flight, 8-mile range, 60 mph top speed, two 4K cameras with 640x optical zoom, two 1280-resolution HD thermal cameras with 64x zoom, a 1,000-lumen SkyBeam phosphor spotlight, and integration with CAD so it can auto-launch on a 911 GPS coordinate**. Resnick's pitch: ["the closest thing to a police helicopter replacement that the drone industry has ever produced"](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/a-former-thiel-fellows-startup-just-launched-a-drone-it-says-can-replace-police-helicopters/), framed against a [$4M+ per-helicopter capex](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/brinc-unveils-guardian-a-starlink-connected-drone-that-could-replace-the-police-helicopter/) and a ["$6-8B market opportunity"](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/a-former-thiel-fellows-startup-just-launched-a-drone-it-says-can-replace-police-helicopters/) (Resnick's math: 20K police departments + 30K fire departments + 80K police/fire stations, half of which will have a 911 drone on the roof). [DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/24/brinc-guardian-drone-starlink-911-response/) frames the Starlink integration as "the move that changes the competitive landscape permanently" because every other DFR platform is cellular-bound and useless in mass-casualty events / earthquakes / floods.

The distribution story is Brinc's real differentiator versus Skydio. Last year Motorola Solutions [invested in Brinc and became the exclusive North American public-safety reseller](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250408177136/en/Motorola-Solutions-Forms-Strategic-Alliances-with-BRINC-and-SkySafe-for-Drone-as-First-Responder-and-Drone-Detection-for-Public-Safety) of its DFR tech, with **integration into CommandCentral Aware** (the 911 command-center software that already runs ~60% of North American PSAPs), **APX NEXT smart-radio emergency-button drone dispatch**, and VESTA 911 / CAD / Real-Time Crime Center hooks. The [Officer Magazine post-Motorola-Summit interview](https://www.officer.com/command-hq/technology/security-surveillance/uav-uas/article/55303898/advancing-dfr) reports 700 agencies actively implementing DFR programs with Brinc. By March 2026 the [Motorola PR](https://www.motorolasolutions.com/newsroom/press-releases/brinc-unveils-guardian-drone-as-first-responder.html) updated that number to **900+ public safety agencies and 20%+ of US SWAT teams** using Brinc products. Revenue tripled in 2025, monthly production quintupled, the new Seattle factory is described by [DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/24/brinc-guardian-drone-starlink-911-response/) as "catching up to orders already placed."

The pushback is the surveillance and incremental-vs-revolutionary debate. ## The Bottom Line

**Choose Skydio if** you want the most-deployed DFR autonomy stack in the U.S., your department already runs Axon body cams, and you can clear the $50K+ X10 + Dock package per node. Skydio's bet this month is that the next five years of U.S. public-safety + defense drones are an industrial-policy story, not a sales story; SkyForge and the Army/Air Force contracts back that. The vulnerability is price-per-capability versus DJI when a department isn't NDAA-constrained.

**Choose Flock if** you already own the LPR cameras and you want Flock911/Aerodome bolted on for near-zero entry cost. The product itself is fine - the problem is that "owning Flock" became a political liability across multiple cities in the same 30-day window, and one Hacker News front page after another keeps amplifying it. A department greenlighting Flock in 2026 should expect to do public-comment work that wasn't on the timeline in 2024.

**Choose Brinc if** you run on Motorola radios + CommandCentral, you need the Starlink-uplink survivability for rural / mass-casualty / disaster ops, or you want the SWAT-grade hardware that ~20% of U.S. SWAT teams already use. Brinc's bet is that distribution (Motorola) plus a true product step-change (Guardian + Starlink) overtakes Skydio's installed base in the segment where helicopter-replacement economics actually pencil. The watch item is whether Guardian's real-world reliability numbers match the spec sheet by end of 2026.

## The emerging stack

The three companies are not converging on a single product - they're converging on the same buyer (the ~20,000 U.S. police departments + 30,000 fire departments) through three different channels: **Skydio + Axon + Dedrone** (autonomy + body-cam + counter-drone), **Brinc + Motorola Solutions + LiveOps** (drone hardware + radio/CAD/911 + DFR ops software), and **Flock LPR + Flock911 + Aerodome** (camera network + drone-as-911-responder). DJI's ~90% global share is being unbundled by U.S. policy (executive orders, federal procurement bans, state-level Chinese-drone bans), and the three companies are racing to be the default in the slice that opens up - while [Paladin Drones](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/11/paladin-drones-8000-dfr-pilot-program/), Aerodome (now inside Flock), [DroneSense](https://www.axon.com/dfr), and Dedrone pick up the layers nobody else wants. Expect 2026 to be the year all three publish per-call response-time data the way Skydio already does (10M calls, Miami Beach 41%/80%). Whoever writes the credible benchmarks first sets the procurement language for 2027.
