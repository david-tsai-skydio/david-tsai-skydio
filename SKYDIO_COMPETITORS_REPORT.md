## Bottom Line

- **Choose Skydio** if you're a buyer in the police / DFR or commercial inspection lane and you care that the vendor's autonomous behavior is publicly documented (Shadow mode = operator clicks target first). The community treats Skydio × Axon as the "closest shipping analogue" to true autonomous DFR while deliberately keeping a human in the loop.
- **Choose Flock** if you can tolerate a sustained, escalating public-trust crisis that has already cost the company its Ring partnership, multiple city contracts, and is generating an active adversarial-tooling ecosystem aimed at mapping, detecting, and destroying its hardware. The brand risk is real and currently growing, not stabilizing.
- **Brinc** does not show up in this signal. Make decisions about Brinc from primary sales / channel data, not from the open-web conversation — there isn't one this window.
