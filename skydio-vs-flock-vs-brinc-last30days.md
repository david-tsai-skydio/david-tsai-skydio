# Skydio vs Flock Safety vs Brinc: What the Community Says (/Last30Days)

## Quick Verdict

The three companies are NOT three flavors of the same product - they are three different bets on the same opportunity (US-made Drone as First Responder), and right now the engagement signal cuts wildly different ways for each. [Skydio](https://www.skydio.com/) is the incumbent ([$4.4B valuation on a $110M Series F](https://www.sourcery.vc/p/breaking-skydio-commits-35b-to-us) in April 2026, [DFR Command at 10M+ processed calls for service](https://www.skydio.com/blog/transparency-drone-as-first-responder), drones first on scene 71% of the time, public-safety customers in 42 of 50 states, [$50M / 3,000-drone US Army order](https://podscan.fm/podcasts/sourcery/episodes/skydio-raises-110m-series-f-at-44b-valuation-ceo-adam-bry), [$3.5B SkyForge commitment](https://www.sourcery.vc/p/breaking-skydio-commits-35b-to-us) to US manufacturing over five years, and at least five new municipal/agency wins announced in the last 30 days alone). [Flock Safety](https://www.flocksafety.com/) is the distribution monster trying to bolt drones onto an ALPR network already in 5,000+ communities - and is simultaneously getting hammered on Hacker News (four of the top last-30-day stories about it are negative, totaling ~1,250 points and ~700 comments). [Brinc](https://brincdrones.com/) is the Seattle-based challenger that just shipped [Guardian](https://brincdrones.com/news/brinc-unveils-guardian-launching-the-next-era-of-drone-as-first-responder/) - the first Starlink-connected DFR drone, with 90-second automated battery swaps and an 8-mile range that more than doubles the non-DJI norm - and has [Motorola Solutions as its exclusive North American reseller](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/24/brinc-guardian-drone-starlink-911-response/). Or as Skydio CEO [Adam Bry](https://shriftman.substack.com/p/skydio-is-americas-drone-prime-a) framed the dynamic to Shriftman: "The market for Dock-based DFR is early, but growing explosively. As such it has attracted new entrants in Brinc and Flock Safety. But Skydio's decade+ headstart and major technology advantages are reflected in market share."

## Skydio

**Community Sentiment:** Positive / Enthusiastic on the operator side (~10 last-30-day press, podcast, and forum mentions across [DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/), [SkydioPilots.com](https://skydiopilots.com/), [Sourcery](https://www.sourcery.vc/), [Shriftman](https://shriftman.substack.com/)); the few discordant notes are about cost and platform lock-in, not technology.

**Strengths (what people love)**
- The X10 + Dock + DFR Command stack is treated as the safe default. [Vanderbilt's campus DFR](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/23/vanderbilt-campus-dfr-skydio-x10/) (live since Commencement), [Bloomington MN's two-week pilot](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/19/bloomington-skydio-x10-dfr-drones-response/) (started May 18), [Leander TX's live-911 trial](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/06/leander-skydio-drones-911/) (May 4 - 15), and the MNPD precinct pilot all landed in May 2026 - per [DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/23/vanderbilt-campus-dfr-skydio-x10/), "when a campus DFR, a metro police pilot, and an Indiana municipal program all converge on the same Skydio platform inside a single quarter, the market is signaling which way the next contract cycle bends."
- Outcome data is now sales material, not a pitch deck. Per [Sourcery](https://www.sourcery.vc/p/breaking-skydio-commits-35b-to-us), an internal analysis of 6,000 flights across 61 agencies shows drone-first-on-scene 71% of the time, 23% of calls cleared without dispatching a unit, helpful intel on 92% of flights, with SFPD crediting the program for a 42% auto theft reduction, 500+ arrests, and a 30% overall crime drop.
- Autonomy and US-made manufacturing are the technical moats operators name. The Australian [Just Drones review](https://justdrones.com.au/skydio-x10-review/) ("the only credible non-DJI commercial drone with genuinely advanced autonomy ... 8-camera nav rig gives the X10 obstacle awareness DJI cant match") echoes a Florida law-enforcement pilot on [SkydioPilots.com](https://skydiopilots.com/threads/skydio-x10-for-public-safety.1688/): "The capabilities and features just seem far greater than any of the other drones out there on the market today ... by far the best I have flown yet."
- Defense business is compounding. The [USAF X10D EOD follow-on contract](https://www.skydio.com/blog/us-air-force-x10d-eod-follow-on-contract) "more than doubles the scope of the initial USAF order announced in November 2025," and Skydio is the platform of record for ACC TACP and PACAF Security Forces.

**Weaknesses (common complaints)**
- Total cost of ownership and ecosystem lock-in. Per [Just Drones](https://justdrones.com.au/skydio-x10-review/): "Full platform with thermal payload, two batteries and Skydio Care lands $20k+ USD. DJI Mavic 3 Enterprise RTK does most of the work at half the cost ... Foundation is a paid tier. Drop the sub, you loose the headline autonomy features."
- 3-mile dock radius is now the floor of the category and competitors are openly pricing above it. [Brinc's Guardian launch material](https://brincdrones.com/news/dfr-3-0-the-next-step-in-the-evolution-of-drone-as-first-responder/) calls out Skydio by name: "a contact-charging platform such as the Skydio X10 requires 35 minutes to recharge from 15% to 95%. In contrast, Guardian's automated battery swap takes 90 seconds."
- Supply constraints. Per [Adam Bry on Sourcery](https://podscan.fm/podcasts/sourcery/episodes/skydio-raises-110m-series-f-at-44b-valuation-ceo-adam-bry): "The number one constraint we're facing right now is building more drones faster ... we will be tripling production over the course of this year."

## Flock Safety

**Community Sentiment:** Strongly negative on Hacker News (4 of the top last-30-day stories about Flock are critical, totaling ~1,250 points / ~700 comments per the [engine pull](https://news.ycombinator.com/from?site=404media.co)) and on local-press / civil-liberties Reddit; cautiously positive in police and city-council quarters, but quietly.

**Strengths (what people love)**
- Distribution and sensor integration are the real product. Per the [Aerodome-Flock partnership announcement](https://www.flocksafety.com/blog/aerodome-and-flock-safety-forge-strategic-partnership-to-expand-drone-as-first-responder-technology-for-public-safety-agencies), Falcon LPR and Raven gunshot sensors automatically dispatch drones, "cutting response times to an average of 86 seconds," with Scottsdale, AZ as the launch city.
- Cash and ambition. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/23/flock-safety-paid-over-300-million-for-17-month-old-drone-startup-aerodome/) confirmed the Aerodome acquisition was over $300M; Garrett Langley promised "a suite of American-made, NDAA-compliant drones" within 12 months. Per Rahul Sidhu (now VP of Aviation), Aerodome is hiring "an additional 100 engineers for the company's aviation team."
- City budgets are sticky. Per [RVA Mag](https://rvamag.com/community/richmond-kept-flock-cameras-in-the-2027-budget.html): Richmond's 2026-2027 budget kept $1.2M in police-tech contracts including Flock "despite widespread criticism during public comment" where "no one spoke in support of it."

**Weaknesses (common complaints)**
- The brand is in active community revolt. The top Flock-tagged story on HN in the window - [404 Media: "At least 25 Flock cameras have been destroyed in five states since April 2025"](https://stateofsurveillance.org/news/flock-cameras-destroyed-nationwide-ice-backlash-2026/) (443 points, 322 comments, May 17) - is literally about people taking the hardware down with hand tools, and the [New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/article/206992/flock-safety-cameras-alpr-deflock-resistance-nationwide) counts ~30 local "DeFlock" groups nationally.
- Trust failures keep landing on the front page. [404 Media: "City Learns Flock Accessed Cameras in Children's Gymnastics Room as a Sales Demo"](https://www.404media.co/city-learns-flock-accessed-cameras-in-childrens-gymnastics-room-as-a-sales-pitch-demo-renews-contract-anyway/) (478 points, 122 comments, May 1) and [Skip Descant on HN: "Flock cameras keep telling police a man who doesn't have a warrant has a warrant"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nHwxV0Sd9V8) (192 points, 145 comments, May 1) hit in the same week.
- Local-government backlash is now an actual policy frontier. [404 Media: "After Town Bans Flock, Councilmember Crashes Out, Proposes Internet, Phone Ban"](https://www.404media.co/after-town-bans-flock-councilmember-crashes-out-proposes-internet-and-phone-ban/) (119 points, 109 comments, May 20) plus [Evanston RoundTable](https://evanstonroundtable.com/2025/09/24/flock-safety-reinstalls-evanston-cameras/) (Flock reinstalled removed cameras without authorization, got a cease-and-desist) describe a company whose biggest 2026 risk is municipal contract churn, not engineering.
- ICE data-sharing context is the through-line. Per the [New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/article/206992/flock-safety-cameras-alpr-deflock-resistance-nationwide): "Flock has drawn ire over the past year amid revelations that Immigration and Customs Enforcement is tapping into its data as part of President Trump's mass deportation regime."

## Brinc

**Community Sentiment:** Thin but positive - most engagement is industry press (Police1, DroneXL, CEO Today) covering the Guardian launch and the Victorville rollout; the founder's narrative carries the brand. No meaningful organic Reddit or HN volume in the window.

**Strengths (what people love)**
- Guardian is the most ambitious DFR airframe in the market. Per [Police1](https://www.police1.com/drones/brinc-unveils-starlink-connected-guardian-drone-with-62-minute-flight-time): 62-minute flight time, 60+ mph top speed, Starlink satellite link, IP55 weather rating, paired with Guardian Station, a "robotic docking system that enables automated battery swapping and redeployment." Per [Brinc](https://brincdrones.com/news/dfr-3-0-the-next-step-in-the-evolution-of-drone-as-first-responder/): "Guardian's automated battery swap takes 90 seconds ... can realistically sustain uptime approaching 98 percent."
- Distribution via Motorola is the under-priced edge. Per [DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/24/brinc-guardian-drone-starlink-911-response/): "Motorola Solutions serves as the exclusive North American reseller for Guardian ... Motorola already has procurement relationships with virtually every public safety agency in the United States." Guardian dispatch can be triggered from APX NEXT radios and from CommandCentral Aware's "Assist AI" via 911 keyword detection ("heart attack," "allergic reaction").
- The mission story sells. Per [CEOWORLD](https://ceoworld.biz/2025/03/18/from-vision-to-revolution-how-blake-resnicks-brinc-drones-is-transforming-emergency-response/): Resnick (Thiel Fellow, Forbes 30u30) founded Brinc after the 2017 Las Vegas mass shooting; per [CEO Today](https://www.ceotodaymagazine.com/2026/01/blake-resnicks-brinc-drones-takes-on-chinese-drone-dominance/), 80% of US public safety still runs Chinese hardware and only 7% runs Brinc - a market gap that's tightening fast.
- Footprint is real. Per [DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/24/brinc-guardian-drone-starlink-911-response/): "More than 900 public safety agencies currently use BRINC products. Over 20% of SWAT teams in the US run BRINC platforms. Newport Beach committed $2.17 million to a BRINC DFR network earlier in 2025." Tripled revenue in 2025; 5x monthly production capacity.

**Weaknesses (common complaints)**
- Operational proof is small relative to the marketing. Per [Adam Bry to Shriftman](https://shriftman.substack.com/p/skydio-is-americas-drone-prime-a): "hundreds of agencies appear to be running Skydio dock-based DFR ... I could only find a small handful of agencies that seem to be running actual operations with competitor products." Per [DroneXL on Victorville](https://dronexl.co/2026/04/27/brinc-drones-victorville-832k/): the $832K three-year contract "almost certainly started with Responder units in the field" rather than Guardian.
- Price gap remains. Per [DroneXL on Guardian](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/24/brinc-guardian-drone-starlink-911-response/): "BRINC's platforms cost significantly more than DJI equivalents even after tariffs narrowed the gap. By end of 2026, Guardian's first real-world operational performance data will become the primary sales tool BRINC uses in every agency conversation it has."
- Endurance numbers are manufacturer-claimed and unverified. Per [DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/24/brinc-guardian-drone-starlink-911-response/): "62 minutes (manufacturer-specified; endurance with payloads loaded has not been independently confirmed)."

## The Bottom Line

**Choose Skydio if** you want the proven, deployed-at-scale DFR stack today. [Adam Bry's transparency post](https://www.skydio.com/blog/transparency-drone-as-first-responder) frames the pitch: "DFR is used on less than 1% of the 911 calls in the US today. How much safer will we all be when that number is 10, 20, or 30%?" - and Skydio is the one with the data to back the curve, per [Sourcery](https://www.sourcery.vc/p/breaking-skydio-commits-35b-to-us): "drone arriving first on scene 71% of the time, 23% of calls cleared without dispatching a patrol unit, and helpful intelligence provided on 92% of flights."

**Choose Flock Safety if** your agency already runs Falcon cameras and Raven sensors and wants the sensor-trigger -> drone-dispatch loop as one procurement, with the Aerodome team handling aviation. But understand the political surface area: per [the New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/article/206992/flock-safety-cameras-alpr-deflock-resistance-nationwide), "DeFlock.me, which hosts a crowdsourced map of ALPRs and warns readers that the cameras are 'a serious risk to your privacy and civil liberties'" is a real and growing movement, and four of the top last-30-day Hacker News stories about Flock are negative.

**Choose Brinc if** your incident profile needs >3-mile reach, Starlink connectivity for rural / coverage-poor areas, or you already buy from Motorola Solutions. Per [Police1](https://www.police1.com/drones/brinc-unveils-starlink-connected-guardian-drone-with-62-minute-flight-time): "Guardian is capable of 62 minutes of flight time and a top speed of more than 60 mph ... allowing it to maintain a data link in areas where traditional infrastructure may be unavailable." And per [DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/24/brinc-guardian-drone-starlink-911-response/), the Motorola channel "covers virtually every public safety agency in North America."

## The emerging stack

The community pattern that's crystallizing in the research window is "American-made NDAA drones + sensor-triggered dispatch + recurring-software contract" - and the three companies are racing toward it from different starting points. Skydio is the autonomy-and-airframe leader extending into software (DFR Command, Extend APIs, Axon integration) and federal defense (USAF EOD, Army $50M). Flock is the sensor-network leader buying its way into airframes (Aerodome) and promising NDAA hardware "over the next 12 months." Brinc is the hardware specialist that just leapfrogged on range (8 miles), connectivity (Starlink), and turnaround (90-second battery swap) and is renting Motorola's existing public-safety distribution rails. Per [Adam Bry on Shriftman](https://shriftman.substack.com/p/skydio-is-americas-drone-prime-a), the macro is that "the market for Dock-based DFR is early but growing explosively" while DFR penetration of 911 calls is still under 1% - which is why all three are still raising, hiring, building factories, and not yet really losing customers to each other so much as collectively converting the 80%+ of US agencies still on Chinese hardware. The last-30-day evidence does not show a winner; it shows the category locking in around a US-made stack that did not exist 24 months ago.
